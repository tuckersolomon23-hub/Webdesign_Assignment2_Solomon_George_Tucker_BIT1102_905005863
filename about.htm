About Us

Our Story

The Good Food Restaurant was founded with a simple idea: to bring people together through great food. We are a small, family-run restaurant at 12 Main Street, Freetown.

We started with a passion for fresh ingredients, local flavours and excellent service, and that is still how we cook every day. Students, workers, families and visitors all have a seat at our table.

Our Mission

To serve high quality, fresh and delicious food while creating a warm and memorable dining experience for every customer.

Our Values

Fresh Ingredients

We buy fresh produce from local markets every morning.

Quality Service

Every plate and every order is checked before it leaves us.

Customer Focus

Your happiness matters. We listen and we improve.

Community

We support our local community and the farmers who feed it.
